The knowledge

Four pillars to live life with wisdom

Gurudev describes four foundations for walking the path of knowledge. The Spiritual Sojourn is your chance to hear them from the Master himself.

01

Viveka

विवेक
Discrimination

The ability to see what is changing and temporary, and what never changes: the Self.

02

Vairagya

वैराग्य
Dispassion

Moving beyond the feverishness of desire into a broader vision of life, with the patience to follow your true passion.

03

Shatt-Sampatti

षट् संपत्ति
The six virtues

Six inner treasures that make you steady and joyful:

Kshama · inner peaceDama · mastery of the sensesTitiksha · enduranceShraddha · faithUparati · wholehearted joySamadhana · contentment
04

Mumukshutva

मुमुक्षुत्व
Intense longing

The deep yearning for the ultimate truth, for freedom, for liberation. If you feel it, this is where it leads.
